The Property

A hundred-year-old cottage with nothing left undone.

"744 square feet, renovated to the last inch, on a lot you can keep with a Saturday morning."

1614 Hubert Street is a detached one-bedroom cottage built in 1920, in the Ross Avenue Heights section of East Dallas, two blocks off Ross Avenue and a short turn from Greenville Avenue.

It has been fully renovated. Herringbone-patterned floors, Craftsman-style trim and warm wood doors carry the age of the house; the kitchen and the bath are new work. Living and kitchen are open to one another, and built-in cabinetry runs through the house — storage designed in rather than furniture added later.

The kitchen makes every inch count: abundant cabinetry, a large island, stainless steel appliances, an apron-front sink and brass hardware. The bath takes 8 by 15 feet and spends it well — a glass-enclosed walk-in shower with a built-in bench, crisp subway tile, graphic black-and-white tilework, black fixtures and brass accents. The bedroom measures 15 by 15, with two closets and a custom closet system.

What it does not come with is a maintenance list or a set of association rules. There is no HOA. The lot is 2,300 square feet — 46 feet across, 50 feet deep — with off-street parking on a concrete driveway and pad, and solar panels that were installed in 2023. The home is offered fully furnished, which makes it as straightforward a second home or investment purchase as it is a first one.

Worth asking for before you write an offer

Three items decide how this house pencils, and none of them should be assumed: the solar documentation — whether the 2023 system is owned, financed or leased, and what transfers at closing; the short-term rental picture — permitting and the city rules that apply at this address today; and the furniture inventory — exactly what conveys with a home offered fully furnished. Add a structural and foundation inspection, standard for a 1920 house on a pier-and-beam foundation. We will gather the paperwork for you. Request the due-diligence package.
